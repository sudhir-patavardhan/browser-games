GROUPBLIND SPOTS

One of you steps into the mirror. Everyone else picks their words.
One person’s “stubborn” is an opinion — five people choosing it is a portrait.

Who’s in the circle? (3–7)
How it works
  • One player at a time steps into the mirror: they pick 6 words that feel like themselves. Then every other player, one hand-off at a time, privately picks 6 words for them.
  • The reveal is a consensus Johari window: Open (the circle sees it, they claim it), Blind spot (the circle sees it — they didn’t say it), Hidden (claimed, but nobody caught it). A ×4 badge means four raters landed on it independently.
  • Close words count — Warm ≈ Kind, Quiet ≈ Private — every word sits on the Big Five’s thirty facets, so near-matches merge and are scored by how close they are.
  • Each mirror opens right after its round, then the next player steps in — until the whole circle has been seen.

For fun and conversation — a game, not a psychological assessment.
Answers stay on this phone and are never uploaded.
