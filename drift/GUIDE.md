# Drift — the full tour

This used to be printed on the start card. It isn't any more: the card now shows nine seconds of the game
instead, because nobody should have to read this to press Drive. It lives here for anyone who wants it.

**Zombies.** Bounties in wallet cash, **paid at your multiplier** — the ×N in the corner of the HUD. Take a
head while you're cruising and it pays the base rate; take one while you're holding a chain and it pays that
many times over, and the popup says so. *Runners* sprint for your lane and pay 2× on top, *brutes* soak two
rounds and pay 3×. Brushing past one at speed is a *close shave*: it feeds the multiplier and keeps a chain
alive between corners. Every couple of klicks the radio calls a *bounty wave* — double pay again for 18 s,
five heads clears it for +$120.

**The road isn't yours.** Deer break from the verge without warning — read one and dodge it clean for the
same nerve a shave pays. And the far lane has an owner: cars, vans and flatbeds come the other way at
closing speed. They'll pull over and lean on the horn for you; take their half anyway and thread it for a
*close call*, or meet one head-on and lose your speed, a bite of the pack, and the chain you were holding.

**Contracts.** Three county jobs a run, top-left under your wallet — cross a target and the cash lands on
the spot.

**Today's Road.** One seeded road a day: same corners, horde and jobs for everyone, with its own best that
resets at midnight. Your best run comes back as a *ghost* to race — and some days it *rains*.

**The wallet.** Buys guns that auto-fire at shamblers on the verge, then garage hardware — a longer-range
pack, a regen tune, track tyres — fitted once, driven on for good. Both are on the start card, folded under
**WALLET · GARAGE · SHELF**.

**Rest areas.** Services at the *3rd, 7th, 12th… km*, signed from 3 km out — a broad exit road into a big
charging lot. Turn in (or hit Pause) and the car takes over: down to *10 km/h*, into a stall, plug in. The
meter bills your *wallet* by the kWh — $0.45/kWh plus a $1 hookup, idle fees and all — and RESUME sees you
back onto the road.

**Trophies.** Firsts and feats land on the shelf, for keeps — and driving Today's Road day after day builds
a *streak* it pays out on.

**The cabin.** You drive from behind the wheel; *V* glances down at a top view. The wheel's left thumb pad
holds cruise, the right skips a track, and the dash stereo plays your own music — tap it, or keys *K* / *N*
/ *P* / *M*.

**Listen to it.** One gear, so the motor's pitch *is* your speed; how loud it is, is how hard you're
pulling. Lift and brake and the note bends down as the pack takes charge back. And the second you're off
the tarmac the road goes loud and dark under you.
