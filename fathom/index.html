36 questions deep — a guided dive for two.

How it works
  • Check in first. Each of you privately picks the picture of two circles that feels like the two of you right now.
  • Then dive. 15 cards across three depths — Surface, Below, Deep. You both answer every card out loud, taking turns going first.
  • Dive tokens. Each of you holds 2 — spend one to swim past a card and draw a fresh one from the same depth.
  • At the bottom you check in again. The reveal is how far the circles moved.
For fun and conversation — a game, not a psychological assessment.
Answers stay on this phone and are never uploaded.
