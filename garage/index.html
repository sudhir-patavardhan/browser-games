How to play

🔧 Drag a part from the tray onto the car — or tap a part, then tap where it goes. Dashed outlines show every open position; work out which is whose.

🔒 A locked part needs another part fitted first — the tray tells you which.

✕ A wrong drop counts as a mistake. Miss the same part twice and its true home starts pulsing.

🎓 Once the car is built, take the quiz: name the glowing part, or tap the part that does the job described.
