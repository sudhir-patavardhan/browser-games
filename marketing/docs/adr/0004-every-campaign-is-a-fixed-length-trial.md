# Every Campaign is a fixed-length Trial; none runs indefinitely

With a twenty-five-dollar daily ceiling, an always-on Campaign would occupy most of the budget and starve experimentation. We decided every Campaign runs a three-day Trial and always ends; a good result is expressed as a Winner label in its Post-mortem, which makes the same game and angle eligible for a fresh, larger Campaign proposed by the Media Buyer. Nothing extends or scales a Campaign in place.

**Consequence:** a Campaign ending while it performs well is the design, not a bug. The follow-up is a new Campaign with its own Review.
