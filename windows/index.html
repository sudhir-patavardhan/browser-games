WINDOWSa Johari game

WINDOWS

How you see yourself vs how they see you.
Two people, one phone, forty words.

How it works
  • Each of you privately picks 6 words for yourself, then 6 for the other player. Your picks collect in a tray at the top — tap one there to drop it. Pass the phone in between — nothing shows until you're both done.
  • Then one page, numbers first: how clearly you see each other, how warm each of you paints the other versus yourself, and both windows — Open (you both picked it), Hidden (only you claimed it), Blind spot (only they see it), and Unknown.
  • Close words count. Forty words overlap a lot, so a pair that means nearly the same thing — Warm ≈ Kind, Quiet ≈ Private, Spontaneous ≈ Impulsive — is scored as a match, weighted by how close they are.
  • The blind spots are the conversation. The page ends with three things to ask each other.

For fun and conversation — a game, not a psychological assessment.
Answers stay on this phone and are never uploaded.
