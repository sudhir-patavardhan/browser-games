WINDOWSa Johari game

WINDOWS

How you see yourself vs how they see you.
Two people, one phone, forty words.

How it works
  • Each of you privately picks 6 words for yourself, then 6 for the other player. Pass the phone in between — nothing shows until you're both done.
  • Then both windows open on one page. Four panes each: Open (you both picked it), Hidden (only you claimed it), Blind spot (only they see it), and Unknown.
  • The panes are the conversation — the blind spots especially. The page ends with three things to ask each other.

For fun and conversation — a game, not a psychological assessment.
Answers stay on this phone and are never uploaded.
